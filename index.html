Research Domains

Advancing optical manipulation from fundamental physics to multi-particle orbital dynamics

Circular Optical Tweezers

High-precision confinement and dynamic orbital steering of micro-particles trapped within laser rings, analyzing acceleration regimes during close pairwise encounters.

Laguerre-Gaussian Beams

Generation and propagation of orbital angular momentum (OAM) carrying optical vortices via Spatial Light Modulators (SLM) and specialized phase plates.

Hydrodynamic Coupling

Experimental study of hydrodynamic cross-talk and viscosity-damped interactions when two microscopic particles navigate shared optical trapping landscapes.
