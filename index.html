About Me

I am a researcher and PhD candidate specializing in classical and physical optics, laser beam shaping, and particle dynamics. My research primarily centers on optical trapping mechanisms, dynamics of micro-particles in circular optical traps, and power-efficiency optimization using spatial light modulators (SLM) and specialized phase plates (SPAAPS/FGPAAPS).

Research Interests

Optical Tweezers & Trapping Laguerre-Gaussian (LG) Beams Photonic Crystals Orbital Angular Momentum (OAM) Spatial Light Modulators (SLM) Computational Optics & MATLAB Modeling

Highlighted Research

Dynamics of Orbiting Particles in Circular Optical Traps

Particle Dynamics & Hydrodynamic Interactions

Investigating two-particle encounters in circular optical potential wells. Analyzing localized orbital accelerations and relative hydrodynamic coupling forces during close-proximity interactions.

Comparative Analysis of Structured Light Generation

Experimental Laser Optics

Systematic efficiency benchmarking among Spatial Phase Apertures (SPAAPS), Fractional Graded-Phase Apertures (FGPAAPS), and Phase-only Spatial Light Modulators (SLM) for ring and vortex beam generation.
